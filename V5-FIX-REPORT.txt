ULOOK INTERIOR - V5 FIX REPORT (13 Nov 2026)
Includes everything from V4 (sticky 3D chapters fixed, videos autoplay on scroll, no play buttons).

V5 CHANGES
1. Photo size: 3D walkthrough frames and the domestic / kitchen / commercial /
   architecture photo sliders were fixed portrait boxes with "contain", so landscape
   photos sat small inside cream/dark padding. Each frame now takes its own photo's
   aspect ratio and the photo fills it (no letterbox, no padding).
   Walkthrough frames are also scaled up to offset the 3D perspective shrink.
2. Spacing: section padding on phones reduced (64px -> 40px etc.); scroll length of
   build, plan, spaces, assemble, layers, precision, growth, story, ribbon and every
   photo slider shortened. Page height on 390px phone: 52,871px -> about 47,000px.
3. Tested again on 390x844 and 1365x900: 0 blank frames across 33 sections,
   videos playing without controls, 0 JavaScript errors.
