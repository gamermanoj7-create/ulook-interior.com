ULOOK INTERIOR - V7 FIX REPORT (13 Nov 2026)
Includes V4 (sticky 3D chapters, videos autoplay, no play buttons), V5 (photos fill
frames), V6 (slower chapters, Selected spaces fills screen).

V7 CHANGES
1. Photo sliders (Domestic 38 photos, Kitchens, Architecture, Commercial) were far too
   fast - about 7vh of scroll per photo. Now 22vh per photo (domestic), 34vh (kitchens
   / architecture), 44vh (commercial) on phones, plus a "dwell" motion: each photo
   rests in the centre, then glides to the next.
2. Mobile width: 3D frames, cards and carousels extended past the screen edge, so
   Android browsers zoomed the page out and left a blank strip on the right. Every
   section now clips its own horizontal overflow; measured page width equals screen
   width at 360 and 390 px, even with the html/body clip switched off.

TESTED (Chromium): 33 sections x 4 scroll positions at 390x844 - 0 blank frames;
videos playing with no controls; 0 JavaScript errors at 360 / 390 / 1365.
NOT TESTED: real phone, live Firebase. Not deployed.
