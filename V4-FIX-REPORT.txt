ULOOK INTERIOR - V4 FIX REPORT (13 Nov 2026)
Base: ulook-interior-v3-intro-restored (newest of the four packages). Intro kept as-is.

ROOT CAUSE OF THE BROKEN / BLANK 3D SCROLL
html AND body both had overflow-x:hidden. That turns <body> into its own scroll
container, so every position:sticky 3D stage (walk into home, plan->3D->finished,
spaces, 3D walkthrough, films, assemble ...) could not pin. The stage scrolled away
and the rest of each chapter was an empty navy/cream screen.
FIX: overflow clipping now lives on <html> only (overflow-x:clip where supported,
hidden as fallback); <body> is overflow:visible. Sticky pinning works again.

VIDEOS
- Removed native controls and every visible play / "Tap to play" button.
- Scroll films and the construction film play automatically (muted, inline) when
  they reach the screen and pause when they leave. If a phone blocks autoplay, the
  next touch starts the video silently - no button is shown.
- Native media controls are also hidden by CSS on all browsers.

TESTED (Chromium, scrolled the full page)
- Mobile 390x844 and 360x740, desktop 1365x900: 33 sections, 4 scroll positions
  each; before the fix 8 chapters showed solid blank frames, after the fix 0.
- Videos: filmDV, kitchen, construction - playing, currentTime advancing, no controls.
- Intro finishes, scroll lock is released; no horizontal overflow at
  320 / 390 / 768 / 1365 px; 0 JavaScript errors.

NOT TESTED
- Real iPhone / Android handset and the live Firebase URL (no access from here).
  Please check on a phone after deploy. Not deployed by me.
