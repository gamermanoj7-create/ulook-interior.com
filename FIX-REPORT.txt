ULOOK INTERIOR — SECOND-PASS REPAIR REPORT
Date: 13 November 2026

ISSUES CONFIRMED FROM THE DEPLOYED-SCREENSHOT FEEDBACK

2. Conflicting mobile hero rules forced an oversized 182svh / 1160px minimum section.
3. The hero image rules used object-fit: contain, which can expose large blank sage-colour bands inside photo frames.

3. Make the hero exterior/interior images fill their frames with object-fit: cover and deliberate focal positions.
4. Tighten mobile headline, lead and action spacing.
5. Keep reduced-motion handling intact.
